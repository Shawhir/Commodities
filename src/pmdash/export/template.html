      What this page can't tell you yet

        Prices are monthly averages, so "end of the month" means that month's average price. "Usually" describes how something has tended to affect gold in the past, not what it will do next. The rules and price levels come from your own settings; this section shows where they stand and never tells you what to do.

        Regime

        —

        Up when the efficiency ratio is at least 0.30 and the 12-month return at least +10%; Down at −10% or worse; otherwise Sideways. Rules fixed in section 8.1, judged on monthly average closes. The section 8 study labels regimes on the USD price.

        Key lines

        —

        Judged on closes with a buffer, never on touches. Only confirmed and failed are alerts. With monthly data only, a break beyond the buffer is confirmed on the same close; daily data will separate the two.

        Stretch

        —

        Filled dot: percentile since 1971 (floating-era windows, so the 10-year average starts in 1981). Hollow dot: percentile within the last 10 years. Sample sizes in brackets.
