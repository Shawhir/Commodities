## Section 8 regime study: reproduction

All section 8 figures reproduce from stored data. The conventions that reproduce them
(found by matching, now fixed in code and config) are:

- The backtest window is **1972-08 to 2026-09**: the first month with a full 12-month lookback after the float. It starts in cash, and the first entry counts as a switch.
- A signal on the close of month t sets the position for month t+1. Each switch costs 0.2%. Cash earns 0%.
- CHF results apply the **USD** signal to CHF-priced returns.
- Per-regime results classify month t+1's return by the label at t, annualised geometrically.
- Chop filter: the position is frozen while the label **lagged one month** is Sideways.
- Regime shares are counted from 1971-08.

| Buy and hold USD / CHF CAGR, max DD | 8.0% −62%, 5.0% −65% | 8.02% −62.1%, 5.02% −64.9% |
| 12-1 momentum | 9.1% −36%, 5.7% −43%, 53 switches | 9.10% −36.1%, **5.77%** −42.7%, 53 |

Two figures differ by less than 0.1 point: the CHF momentum CAGR (5.77% here, 5.7% in the
brief) and the Up-regime momentum return (20.69% here, 20.6% in the brief). Everything else
matches to the precision the brief shows.

**New observation:** on the September 2026 close, gold in **CHF** reads **Up** (12-month
return +21%, ER 0.34), while gold in USD reads Sideways (+18%, ER 0.24). The study labels
regimes on the USD price. The weekly summary shows both.
