    Price levels to watch

    —

    A level only counts as crossed when a month's price ends clearly beyond it (by more than 1%), not when it briefly touches it. You get an alert only when a crossing holds, or when the price comes straight back.

      What happened after setups like this

      —

      Counts from history, not a forecast. For each way of describing today, this shows how often gold was higher 3, 6 and 12 months later, compared with all months since 1972. "Separate spells" counts each stretch of similar months once, because neighbouring months share most of their future.

      The honesty test asks: if you had used these counts at the time, using only the history available then, would they have described what came next better than "gold usually rises"? Anything that fails is labelled "for context only".
